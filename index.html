About

Ideas are cheap. Shipping them is the skill.

In one weekend you will go from a rough idea to a working proof of concept. You do not need to know how to code: today's AI tools do the heavy lifting, and mentors are on hand all the way.

You will pick a real-world problem, build fast with no-code tools and AI, and pitch the result to a panel of investors, founders and faculty. You leave with a live demo, new teammates, and the confidence that you can build and launch faster than you thought possible.

What to expect

What you only get here

No-code friendly

You do not need to be an engineer. With AI tools you can ship a prototype by describing it in plain English. The AI does the heavy lifting while you focus on the idea.

From pitch to demo

Most competitions end with slides. Here you end with a clickable, live product. Investors take notice when they can use what you have built.

Investor feedback

Real, practical critique from investors, founders and AI experts. The kind of feedback start-ups hear before they raise money: direct and actionable.

Meet your co-founders

Work alongside students from across UCD, from the Smurfit School to engineering and computer science. Test how well your idea lands with ambitious peers.

Momentum beyond Sunday

Your demo should not die on Sunday evening. The hackathon is a launchpad: winning teams are introduced to UCD's start-up support and to the investors in the room.

A compressed learning curve

In one weekend you learn modern AI workflows, design thinking, rapid prototyping and pitching. Skills that normally take months of trial and error.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Do I need coding experience?
No. The hackathon is designed for everyone. AI and no-code tools will help you ship a working prototype even if you have never written a line of code.
Who can take part?
Any registered UCD student, from the Smurfit School's MBA and MSc programmes to undergraduates across the university. This year the event is for UCD students only.
Can I join without a team?
Yes. Register on your own and we run team-formation activities at kick-off. You are also free to build something alone.
What should I bring?
Your laptop, a charger and your ideas. We provide the workspace, Wi-Fi, meals, snacks and coffee.
How are projects judged?
By a panel of investors, faculty and alumni founders, on clarity of the problem, innovation, quality of the prototype, business potential and the pitch itself.
What can I win?
€5,000 in total prizes, plus exposure to investors and a path into UCD's start-up support for the strongest teams.
Does it cost anything?
No. Taking part is free for UCD students, meals included.
